# Render match handoff (2026-10-14, fix/render-match in wt/render-match, from update/parity-1014 c2b983c)

## Result
- **Root cause found and fixed:** the editor's .pat part perspective was right-handed; the game's part space is
  left-handed (uni2 1.42 `PatPart_GetGlobalPerspective640x480` 0x4C1860, called from `PatPart_BuildTransformMatrix`
  0x4C10E0). Every part with an X/Y rotation tilted the wrong way in depth.
  - `src/parts/part_render_math.h`: `partrender::PartPerspective(w, h)` (z mirrored), used by `Render::UpdateProj`.
  - `src/parts/parts.cpp`: the ring/arc/sphere/cone shapes back in the game's +z sign
    (`CPAniPrimRender_Ring` 0x414120, `_Donuts` 0x414590, `_Sphere` 0x414A60, `_Corn` 0x414DF0). They had been
    stored negated to make up for the old projection.
  - `tests/part_render_math_test.cpp`: `TestPartPerspective` projects points through the game's own D3DX chain and
    through the editor matrix; they agree to 0.05 px.
- PRRV flip was already right: `PatPart_DrawPlaneQuad` 0x4BFCB0 mirrors only the UV.
- ctest 40/40; `tools/uni/uni_regress.sh` UNI_REGRESS_PASS
  (log: `docs/audit_2026_10_14/evidence/compare/v2_uni_regress.log`).
- Per-case status: `docs/audit_2026_10_14/evidence/compare/MISMATCHES.md`, section v2.
  - 12 of 15 match on the full-frame sheets.
  - Open: flipHV (one brown band), PUPS chr027 (black part lifted to (0,21,35) in game), MBTL pral2 chr004 (Last Arc
    cutscene camera).

  - Proven live: with the aura recorded in the sidecar, the prediction is exact; with the aura held at 0, the game equals the editor.
